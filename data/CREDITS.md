# Sentence corpus credits

`sentences.json` contains 43457 Japanese sentences with English
translations, taken from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) and filtered to
those whose every content word is in this project's N5/N4 bank.

Tatoeba's exports are released under
[CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/deed.en),
which requires appropriate credit to the creator - a source link alone
does not satisfy it - so every contributor whose sentence appears is
named below. Each sentence also keeps its Tatoeba id, which resolves to
the original at `https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/<id>`.

13892 sentences have no recorded contributor in Tatoeba's export;
credit for those goes to Tatoeba and its community.
